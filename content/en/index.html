Pull, aim and sling.

Adventure Smash is a turn-based battle game: you fling your monsters like a slingshot, ricochet off the enemies and move up a trail of stages with bosses. Online, free and with no ads.

In closed beta. The game is being tested with a small group of players on Android. Want to join? Write to suporte@adventuresmash.online. Already in? The latest test APK asks for the group's user name and password.

The essentials

  • No sign-up: your account is born from the device itself, with no e-mail and no password.
  • No purchases and no ads during the beta.
  • You can delete your account at any time, from the app or by e-mail.
  • What the game stores and why: privacy policy.
